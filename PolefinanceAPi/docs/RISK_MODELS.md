# Risk models

Implemented risk analytics include annualized volatility, downside deviation, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, maximum/average drawdown, drawdown duration and recovery, historical and Gaussian parametric VaR/CVaR, Monte Carlo VaR/CVaR, beta, alpha, tracking error, information ratio, covariance/correlation, marginal/component/percentage risk contribution, concentration, diversification ratio, rolling volatility/Sharpe/beta/correlation/drawdown, and data-quality guards.
